> Telecommunication is the transmission of information by various types of technologies over wire, radio, optical or other electromagnetic systems. It is also an industry: the operators who build those networks, the spectrum they are licensed to use, and the terms on which they resell access to it.

These posts are a snapshot of the late 2000s, when mobile internet in the Baltics and the Nordics stopped being a novelty and became the connection people actually used. Operators were competing on coverage and megabits, national broadband projects were being signed, and the [iPhone](/iphone) was arriving one exclusive carrier deal at a time. What kept drawing me back was that the interesting part was rarely the handset — it was who controlled the pipe, and on what terms they were willing to share it.

Please find selected telecommunications posts below. Topics vary from mobile network rollouts and broadband build-out to iPhone carrier pricing and the competition scrutiny that followed it.
