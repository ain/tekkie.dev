1. Turn on Caps Lock from **Settings** > **General** > **Enable Caps Lock**:

   <a style="margin-top:20px; margin-bottom:20px; display:block;" title="Enabling Caps Lock from the iPhone Settings" href="/assets/uploads/2009/12/IMG_0727.PNG"><img title="Enabling Caps Lock from the iPhone Settings" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/IMG_0727.PNG" alt="Enabling Caps Lock from the iPhone Settings" width="154" height="230" /></a>

2. Return to your application, double-tap Shift so it turns blue and there you have the Caps Lock in iPhone:

   <a style="margin-top:20px; margin-bottom:20px; display:block;" title="Caps Lock in iPhone Notes" href="/assets/uploads/2009/12/IMG_0726.PNG"><img title="Caps Lock in iPhone Notes" src="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/IMG_0726.PNG" alt="Caps Lock in iPhone Notes" width="154" height="230" /></a>
